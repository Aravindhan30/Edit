HOW IT WORKS

From sensing
to solution.

The festival is designed around a simple engineering flow: understand the physical quantity, select the right sensing principle, interface it with electronics, and turn the data into an action.

01

Sense

Detect a physical quantity such as light, temperature, distance or motion.

02

Process

Condition and interpret the sensor signal using a controller or electronic circuit.

03

Act

Use the information to trigger an alert, display, actuator or smart decision.
